Multi-agent system

Accessible Travel Planning Team

A traveler types one message with route, dates, budget and accessibility needs: for example SFO to Lisbon, 3 nights, $900, a direct flight, wheelchair assistance, and a hotel with a step-free entrance. Five agents turn it into a plan and review it before the traveler sees it.

Business problem

Accessible trips have hard constraints. A plan that looks good but misses one, such as a step-free entrance, is a failed plan.

What the team does

Finds a flight and a hotel from live data sheets, writes the itinerary, then checks it against the traveler’s rules and the weather forecast.

Why it matters to leadership

The output is a clear decision, APPROVED or NEEDS REVISION, instead of an unchecked draft.

Design choice

The agent that writes the plan is not the one that approves it. A separate checker holds the rules.

Retrieval-augmented agent

Product Finance Reporting Agent for NovaCart

Product Finance at NovaCart owes leadership a Monthly Financial Standing Report by the 1st of every month. It means combining internal product sales with external industry outlooks, by hand. This agent retrieves both, correlates them, and emails the report.

Business problem

Manual aggregation across internal databases and external intelligence reports is slow and prone to oversight, and the report drives strategy.

What the agent does

Retrieves internal sales data and external market material, reranks results for relevance, and writes a structured report to the stakeholder.

Why it matters to leadership

A consistent six-section report with explicit priorities, owners and success metrics, not a free-form summary.

Design choice

The agent states what it could not verify instead of filling gaps. That is the behavior leadership needs from a finance tool.

  • n8n
  • Pinecone
  • OpenAI embeddings and chat
  • Cohere reranker
  • Gmail

Report format

  1. Executive summary
  2. Internal financial performance
  3. External market context
  4. Key insights
  5. Strategic recommendations, with priority, success metric and dependency
  6. Data gaps and follow-up actions
